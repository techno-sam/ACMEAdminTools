Additions
- Ban Hammer - hit a player to kill and ban them instantly.
- Boot on a Stick - hit a player to kill and kick them instantly.
- Vorpal Sword - hit a player or an entity to kill it instantly.
- Alchemical Laser - apply potion effects to mobs up to 128 blocks away.
- Doom Potion - kills players when it expires, or if level II, bans them.
- Inhibition Potion - prevents players from moving or interacting with the world
- Marked Potion - tracks a player's location
- Antidote Potion - the only way to cure ACME potion effects
